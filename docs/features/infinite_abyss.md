The infinite abyss is accessible if the player has at least one champion.
The infinite abyss is the same as a dungeon run except monster drop no experience or equipment. Instead, the focus is on surviving as long as possible and achieving high scores.

Champions are consumed upon entering the infinite abyss, meaning they cannot be used again until they are obtained from another dungeon run.
The infinite abyss has a scoring system based on the number of monsters defeated and the duration of survival. Players aim to achieve the highest possible score before their champions are consumed.
The infinite abyss scales the same way regular dungeon runs do, with increasing difficulty with floor levels as players progress further into the abyss.
The infinite abyss starts on floor level 50 and continues indefinitely, with the difficulty increasing as players descend further into the abyss.
Players can bring run items into the infinite abyss to aid them in their journey, providing various benefits such as enhancing combat abilities, offering utility effects, or granting temporary buffs. The choice of run items can significantly impact the player's strategy and success in the abyss.
